## Legacy coverage retained

The approved v10 reference deck was extracted and mapped before rebuilding. The v11 package retains its three-part scope:

1. Introduction to infographics: purpose, formats, planning and insight extraction.
2. Creating infographics in PowerPoint: canvas, guides, colour, fonts, shapes, icons, text, charts and export.
3. Reviewing infographics: storytelling, do-and-don't guidance, audience outcomes and improvement.

The v11 deck is rebuilt as editable courseware rather than copying raster slides. Detailed procedures are placed in the Learner Guide and activity instructions, not in the presentation.

## New coverage

- Microsoft Infographic Maker: editable templates, visual assets, SmartArt, charts, Excel data, branding, collaboration and export.
- Microsoft AI Presentation Designer / Copilot: layout generation and refinement, AI imagery, brand alignment and human review.
- Native chart evidence chain: data, context, chart, title, update control and ownership.
- Responsible AI review gate: truth, privacy, rights, accessibility, brand and accountable release.
- Resource and investment evaluation: capability, total cost, effectiveness and risk.
