# Activity 07 — Peer Coaching and Story Review

- **Course:** Infographics and Data Visualization with PowerPoint (TGS-2026064178)
- **Duration:** 30 minutes
- **Tools:** PowerPoint + peer review workbook
- **Output:** Peer-review log, revised infographic and 150-250 word reflection

## Objective

Give specific criteria-based feedback and revise an infographic to improve clarity, story and accessibility.

## Scenario

Each learner presents one infographic from Activities 3–6 to two peers. The group must distinguish aesthetic preference from evidence-based review.
Capture two observations, two strengths, two improvements and one agreed revision. Then revise the master and record the change.

## Detailed instructions

1. **Choose the review slide.** Open one infographic from Activities 3-6 and save a copy for revision.

2. **Assign roles.** Work in groups of three: Designer, Reviewer and Audience representative. Rotate roles if time permits.

3. **Run the five-second test.** Show the slide for five seconds, then hide it. The audience representative states the message, evidence and intended action they remember.

4. **Record observations.** In A07-Peer-Coaching-and-Story-Review-Data.xlsx capture visible facts only: reading order, text size, labels, contrast, source, chart comprehension and action clarity.

5. **Ask clarifying questions.** The reviewer asks which audience, message, evidence and constraint drove the design. The designer answers without dismissing the observation.

6. **Apply the six-lens rubric.** Score Message, Data, Hierarchy, Accessibility, Story and Channel fit. Add one evidence note for every score.

7. **Prioritise one change.** Choose the issue with the largest audience or decision impact. State the change, owner and acceptance check.

8. **Revise the master.** Update the editable PPTX. Do not flatten the file or create an uncontrolled duplicate.

9. **Retest and reflect.** Repeat the five-second test and write 150-250 words on what improved, what remains and why.

## Suggested timing

- 5 minutes — Assign roles and run five-second test
- 10 minutes — Observe, question and score
- 8 minutes — Prioritise and revise
- 5 minutes — Retest
- 2 minutes — Save reflection

## Included inputs and assets

- Editable PowerPoint start and worked-example files
- PROMPTS.md with copy-ready data-analysis, infographic, Gemini Notebook and text-to-image prompts
- AI Prompt Lab worksheet for provenance and human-change records

## Submission

Submit the editable PPTX, completed XLSX, source CSV, prompt/source log, used assets and checklist. Record every AI tool, prompt, source, rejected claim and material human change.

## Debrief

The review log contains observed evidence, a principle-based diagnosis, one prioritised change, a named owner and an acceptance check.
