# Activity 06 — Generate and Refine an AI-Assisted Infographic

**Course:** Infographics and Data Visualization with PowerPoint (TGS-2024048310)  
**Duration:** 45 minutes  
**Tools:** Copilot / AI Designer + PowerPoint  
**Output:** Two AI layout options, reviewed final slide and completed prompt/source log

## Objective

Use copilot or ai designer to generate options and apply a human review gate before release.

## Scenario

Northstar wants an executive one-slide infographic summarising the sustainable-delivery pilot. Copilot or AI Designer may generate a layout, section titles and supporting image ideas from the approved source pack.
Generate two options, compare them, then rebuild the selected direction into an editable, verified and accessible PowerPoint slide.

## Detailed instructions

1. **Open the prompt brief.** Open A06-AI-Infographic-Prompt-and-Log.xlsx. Complete Audience, objective, approved source data, infographic type, brand rules, accessibility rules and forbidden claims.

2. **Start Copilot or AI Designer.** In PowerPoint select the Copilot button or open the Microsoft AI presentation designer page. Use only an approved account and approved synthetic data.

3. **Submit a structured prompt.** Ask for a single-slide executive infographic with two layout options. Require no invented data, explicit source space, editable content, restrained brand colours and a clear recommendation.

4. **Capture both options.** Save screenshots or duplicate the generated slides. Label them Option A and Option B; do not overwrite the original output.

5. **Score the options.** Use the Review sheet to score message accuracy, hierarchy, data integrity, editability, brand, accessibility and output fit.

6. **Verify every claim.** Compare every number, label and relationship with A06-Pilot-Summary-Data.xlsx. Delete or correct anything not supported.

7. **Rebuild critical evidence.** Create native PowerPoint text, shapes and charts for all decision-critical content. Replace any flattened chart or unreadable generated text.

8. **Check imagery and rights.** For AI images record the prompt, intended use and review result. Do not treat a generated conceptual image as measured evidence.

9. **Run accessibility review.** Use Review > Check Accessibility. Fix reading order, contrast, missing alt text and reliance on colour alone.

10. **Record ownership.** Complete the prompt/source log with tool, date, approved inputs, human changes, reviewer and final owner.

## Suggested timing

- 7 minutes — Complete the structured prompt
- 8 minutes — Generate two options
- 10 minutes — Score and verify
- 15 minutes — Rebuild and check accessibility
- 5 minutes — Complete log and submit

## Submission

Submit the editable PPTX, completed XLSX, source CSV and checklist. Record any AI tool, prompt, source and material human change.

## Debrief

The final slide records the prompt, sources and human changes; all claims are verified and the decision evidence remains editable.
