# Activity 06 — Copy-Ready AI Prompts

Use these prompts with only approved or synthetic inputs. Replace bracketed details, keep a copy of the submitted prompt, and verify every output against the source pack.

## Data analyst

Using only the attached approved sources, create a source register, identify duplicates or conflicting definitions, clean the table, show formulas or transformations, and return five candidate insights with citations, caveats and a confidence note. Do not invent missing values.

**Verification:** Record sources used, claims checked, rejected output, human changes, reviewer and owner.

## Infographic

Create a one-page executive infographic for Singapore retail managers. Lead with one decision insight, show three supporting metrics, include a five-stage flow and a source footer. Use a clean professional style, strong hierarchy and no invented data. Leave critical labels editable.

**Verification:** Record sources used, claims checked, rejected output, human changes, reviewer and owner.

## Text to image

Create a clean vector-like sustainability delivery illustration for a PowerPoint infographic: one electric van, one reusable parcel, a simple route and a feedback symbol. Transparent background, consistent blue/teal/violet/amber palette, no text, no logo, no watermark.

**Verification:** Record sources used, claims checked, rejected output, human changes, reviewer and owner.

## Gemini Notebook deck

Create Presenter Slides for business stakeholders. Focus on the verified decision insight, use a concise professional style, include source-grounded comparisons and a final recommendation. Avoid claims not supported by selected sources. Download PowerPoint when available; otherwise download PDF and identify what must be rebuilt as editable native PowerPoint objects.

**Verification:** Record sources used, claims checked, rejected output, human changes, reviewer and owner.

## Prompt anatomy

Role and task → audience and decision → approved sources and evidence → output structure → visual style and format → constraints and forbidden claims → verification and ownership.
