## Legacy coverage retained

The approved v10 reference deck and v11 rebuilt package were mapped before this revision. v12 retains the approved three-part scope:

1. Introduction to infographics: purpose, formats, audience, planning and insight extraction.
2. Creating infographics in PowerPoint: canvas, hierarchy, colour, type, shapes, icons, SmartArt, native charts, Excel data and export.
3. Reviewing infographics: evidence, story, accessibility, audience outcomes, resource decisions and improvement.

Detailed click-by-click procedures remain in the Learner Guide and activity folders. The PPT remains concept-led and visual.

## v12 AI-first coverage

- Source-to-insight workflow: collect approved files, URLs, transcripts and datasets; clean and reconcile data; analyse patterns; extract show–mean–matter–act insights; verify claims and calculations.
- Microsoft 365 Copilot: generate an infographic from a detailed prompt and approved file, then edit or download the result subject to account availability.
- Gemini Notebook: select notebook sources, generate and customise an Infographic, generate Presenter Slides, download the Slide Deck as `.pptx` where available, or preserve the PDF and rebuild decision-critical content as native PowerPoint objects.
- Tool comparison: apply the same brief and evidence to Microsoft 365 Copilot and Gemini Notebook, then score source grounding, accuracy, hierarchy, editability, brand, accessibility, speed and governance fit.
- Text-to-image assets: generate a consistent visual family with no embedded factual text; retain prompt/provenance and place all evidence as editable PowerPoint objects.
- Copy-ready prompts: data cleaning/analysis, infographic generation, text-to-image assets and Gemini Notebook-to-PowerPoint narrative.
- Human review gate: truth, privacy, rights, accessibility, brand, editability, provenance and accountable ownership.
